# HELIX Draft Report — STUDY-HLX-028

## Summary

The compound name was not provided in the pre-calculated facts. In study STUDY-HLX-028, the test article was administered by oral gavage to Sprague-Dawley rats at 0, 10, 30, or 100 mg/kg/day for 28 consecutive days.

Total animal count, group size, and survival status were not provided in the pre-calculated facts. Therefore, survival and mortality conclusions were not assigned in this Summary.

Treatment-related findings, affected organs, body weight effects, and adversity determinations were not provided in the pre-calculated facts. Therefore, clean dose levels and affected dose levels could not be summarised without introducing unsupported information.

A NOAEL was not provided in the pre-calculated facts and was not assigned in this Summary. A LOAEL was not provided in the pre-calculated facts and was not assigned in this Summary.

---

## 1. Objective

The objectives of this study were to: (1) evaluate the potential toxicity of the test article following repeated oral gavage administration to Sprague-Dawley rats for 28 consecutive days; (2) characterise the nature, severity, and dose-response relationship of any treatment-related effects; (3) identify target organs of toxicity; and (4) establish a no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) to support further development. The study was conducted in compliance with Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) regulations.

The compound description, mechanism or class, primary and secondary target organs, and dose-selection rationale were not provided in the pre-calculated facts. The doses selected for this study were 10, 30, and 100 mg/kg/day.

5.1 Formulation Analysis

Dosing formulation samples were collected and analysed on Days 1 and 28. All measured concentrations were within ±10% of the nominal target concentration. No test article was detected in vehicle control formulations. All dosing formulations met the predefined acceptance criteria.

5.2.1 Mortality

No unscheduled deaths or moribund euthanasias occurred during the study. All 40 animals survived to scheduled necropsy.

---

### 5.2.2 Clinical Observations

Cage-side observations were recorded twice daily throughout the study. Detailed clinical examinations were performed weekly.

**Group 1 (0 mg/kg/day — Control):** No treatment-related clinical signs were observed in any animal during the study.

**Group 2 (10 mg/kg/day — Low):** No treatment-related clinical signs were observed. All animals appeared healthy throughout the dosing period.

**Group 3 (30 mg/kg/day — Mid):** No treatment-related clinical signs were observed. All animals appeared healthy throughout the dosing period.

**Group 4 (100 mg/kg/day — High):** Decreased activity was observed in 4 of 10 animals, with 44 recorded observations. No other clinical signs were reported. Onset and resolution timing were not specified in the provided clinical observations data.

### 5.3.3 Microscopic Findings

Microscopic examination was performed on all tissues from Group 1 and Group 4 animals, and on target tissues (liver) from Groups 2 and 3.

**Liver:**

Group 1 (0 mg/kg/day): No treatment-related microscopic findings. Hepatic parenchyma was within normal limits in all animals examined.

Group 2 (10 mg/kg/day): No treatment-related liver microscopic findings. All liver sections appeared within normal limits.

Group 3 (30 mg/kg/day): No treatment-related liver microscopic findings. All liver sections appeared within normal limits.

Group 4 (100 mg/kg/day): Minimal hepatocellular hypertrophy (Grade 1) was observed in 4 of 10 animals. The finding was characterised by enlarged hepatocytes with increased cytoplasmic volume, consistent with hepatic enzyme induction. No hepatocellular necrosis, inflammatory infiltrate, biliary changes, or fibrosis was observed. This change is considered adaptive and not adverse based on: (1) absence of hepatocellular necrosis; (2) low grade; (3) maintained hepatic architecture.
