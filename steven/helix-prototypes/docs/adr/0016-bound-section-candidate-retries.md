# Bound section candidate retries

When provenance compilation or Template Conformance Gates reject a Section Draft Candidate, HELIX returns structured failures to the same Section Agent for at most two revisions after the initial attempt. A retry may change only wording, structure, and claim placement while retaining the same frozen manifest, Validated Claims, Section Package, template contract, and governed versions; it cannot alter evidence, rules, provenance, or dependencies. Every candidate and executor receipt remains preserved, and if the third attempt fails, automation stops for that section and the versioned Review Scaffold records `[NEEDS REVIEW]`. An explicit Human-Directed Revision starts a new, separately audited three-attempt cycle for only the selected section.
