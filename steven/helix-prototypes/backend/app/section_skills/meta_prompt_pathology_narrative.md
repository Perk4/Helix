# Meta-Prompt: Anatomic Pathology Narrative Style
# Extracted from: approved_report_1.md, approved_report_2.md, approved_report_3.md
# Scope: Global — applies to all sections of every HELIX-generated report

---

## Voice and Person

✅ Passive, impersonal throughout
  "was observed", "were identified", "is considered", "was performed"
❌ Never active first-person
  "we found", "I noted", "the team observed", "our data shows"

---

## Tense

✅ Past tense for all results and observations — the study is complete
  "Minimal tubular basophilia was observed..."
  "Body weight was reduced at Group 4..."
✅ Present tense only for standing conclusions in Section 5.3.4
  "The NOAEL is 10 mg/kg/day."
  "The finding is considered adaptive."
❌ Never mix tenses within a paragraph

---

## Incidence Format

✅ "X of Y males", "X of Y females", "all 5 males", "all 5 females"
✅ Both sexes on one line when different: "3 of 5 males and 2 of 5 females"
✅ Single sex affected: "3 of 5 males" — do not add females if none affected
❌ Never percentages: "60% of males"
❌ Never vague quantifiers: "most animals", "several females", "a few males"
❌ Never "animals" when sex is known — always state sex

---

## Grading Format

✅ Grade stated in parentheses immediately after finding name
  "tubular basophilia (Grade 1)"
  "hepatocyte vacuolisation (Grade 2)"
✅ Grade range when spanning animals: "Grade 1 to 2"
✅ Grade on first mention in paragraph — not repeated on each sentence
❌ Never: "mild tubular basophilia" without parenthetical grade
❌ Never: "Grade 1 tubular basophilia" — grade follows name, not precedes

---

## Group Label Format

✅ Full label on first mention in each section: "Group 4 (300 mg/kg/day)"
✅ Abbreviated on subsequent mentions within same section: "Group 4" or "at 300 mg/kg/day"
✅ Always group number + dose together on first mention — never dose alone without group

---

## Adversity Determination Language

✅ State the determination — never hedge it
  "This finding is considered adaptive and not adverse."
  "This finding is considered treatment-related and adverse at 150 mg/kg/day."
✅ Provide explicit numbered criteria for adaptive calls
  "...based on: (1) absence of necrosis; (2) no macroscopic correlate; (3) low incidence."
✅ Reference correlates for adverse calls
  "...in the context of associated organ weight increases and body weight reduction."
❌ Never hedge: "may be adaptive", "possibly not significant", "appears to be non-adverse"
❌ Never assert adaptive without criteria — the determination must be defensible

---

## Absence Documentation

✅ Explicitly state what was NOT seen — this is as important as positive findings
  "No tubular degeneration, necrosis, cast formation, or inflammatory infiltrate was observed."
  "No macroscopic correlate was identified."
✅ Absence statements follow the positive finding description — always the last sentence before adversity
❌ Never omit absence documentation for an adaptive determination
❌ Never use "no significant changes" — always name the specific things absent

---

## Group Ordering

✅ Always describe groups lowest dose to highest: G1 → G2 → G3 → G4
✅ Always include Group 1 baseline — even one sentence: "Renal parenchyma was within normal limits."
✅ Do not skip clean groups — state "No treatment-related findings" explicitly
❌ Never start with the high-dose group and work backwards

---

## Organ Weight Language

✅ State absolute value and percentage change together: "2.33 g vs. 2.20 g control; +5.8%"
✅ State statistical result: "did not reach statistical significance (p > 0.05, Dunnett's test)"
  or "statistically significant (p < 0.01, Dunnett's test)"
✅ Note both sexes when both affected: "+18.4% males, +16.2% females"
❌ Never state percentage change without the direction (+ or −)
❌ Never state statistical significance without naming the test used

---

## Body Weight Language

✅ Express as percentage vs. control at the terminal timepoint
  "Group 4 males were −15.0% relative to controls at Week 13"
✅ Note week/day of maximum difference
❌ Never state body weight reduction without specifying when it was maximal

---

## Reversibility Language (Recovery Groups)

✅ State finding status at recovery necropsy per tissue
  "Full reversal was confirmed at Day 120."
  "The infiltrate was substantially reduced (Grade 1, 2 of 5 males), indicating partial reversibility."
  "No residual findings were observed in any recovery animal."
❌ Never summarise reversibility only in the Summary — state it at the tissue level in 5.3.3

---

## Mortality Language

✅ Name the animal ID, day of death, and cause
  "Male M101 was found moribund on Day 35 and humanely euthanised."
✅ State treatment relationship explicitly
  "This death is considered incidental to treatment." or "...considered compound-related."
❌ Never state "one animal died" without naming the animal ID and the day

---

## Conclusion Language (Section 5.3.4)

✅ State NOAEL with the highest dose label: "The NOAEL is X mg/kg/day, the highest dose tested."
  or "The NOAEL is X mg/kg/day."
✅ State LOAEL if established: "The LOAEL is X mg/kg/day based on [specific finding]."
✅ State when no LOAEL: "No LOAEL was established."
✅ Link NOAEL to the finding basis: "No adverse findings were identified at X mg/kg/day."
❌ Never state NOAEL without linking it to the absence of adverse findings at that dose
❌ Never state "NOAEL could not be determined" — state what was and was not found, let the conclusion follow
