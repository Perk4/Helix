# Skill: Section 5.3.2 — Macroscopic Observations
# Sources: approved_report_1, approved_report_2, approved_report_3
# Primary data source: gross_pathology.csv

## Purpose
Reports gross post-mortem examination findings per group. If all groups are clean, this is stated collectively. If findings are present, they are described per group with organ, appearance, and incidence. This section sets the context for microscopic findings — adverse macroscopic changes are important correlates for adversity determinations in 5.3.3.

## Structure (always in this order)
1. One sentence: examination scope and timing
2. Group-by-group findings (G1 → G2 → G3 → G4)
3. Recovery group findings (only if recovery groups present)

## Examination Statement
"A complete macroscopic examination was performed on all animals at scheduled necropsy on Day [X]."

## Clean Group Template (most common)
"Group [N] ([dose] mg/kg/day): No treatment-related macroscopic findings were observed. All tissues examined appeared grossly normal."

Or for all groups collectively when all clean:
"Groups 1, 2, 3, and 4 (0, 15, 75, and 300 mg/kg/day): No treatment-related macroscopic findings were observed in any tissue, including the kidneys, liver, spleen, thymus, adrenal glands, lungs, heart, brain, or gonads, in any male or female animal at any dose level. All tissues examined appeared grossly normal."

## Findings Template
"Group [N] ([dose] mg/kg/day): [Organ] appeared [description] in [X of Y males / all males / A of B females]. [Additional finding if present]. No treatment-related macroscopic findings were observed in any other organ or tissue."

## Descriptor Language (use approved pathology gross terms)
✅ "pale and enlarged" (kidneys), "dark red-brown with multifocal consolidation" (lungs)
✅ "small" (thymus, adrenal), "mottled" (liver), "pale" (liver, kidney)
❌ Never use: "abnormal", "unusual", "different", "concerning"

## Recovery Group Template
"Recovery animals (Group [N]R and Group [M]R): At Day [X] necropsy, no residual macroscopic findings were observed in Group [N]R recovery animals. [Organ] appeared grossly normal in all recovery animals."

## What Must Not Be Missing
- All four groups must be addressed — even one sentence for clean groups
- Named tissues must appear in clean group statements for completeness
- If G4 had macroscopic findings, those must be named here — they will be referenced as correlates in 5.3.3
- Recovery group outcome must be stated if recovery animals exist

## Example — All Groups Clean (from approved_report_3)
"Groups 1, 2, 3, and 4 (0, 15, 75, and 300 mg/kg/day): No treatment-related macroscopic findings were observed in any tissue, including the kidneys, liver, spleen, thymus, adrenal glands, lungs, heart, brain, or gonads, in any male or female animal at any dose level. All tissues examined appeared grossly normal. The minimal increase in kidney weight observed in Group 4 animals was not associated with any macroscopic correlate."

## Example — Findings at G4 (from approved_report_2)
"Group 4 (150 mg/kg/day): Macroscopic findings were confined to the lungs and thymus. The lungs of 8 of 10 males and 7 of 10 females appeared dark red-brown with multifocal consolidation affecting predominantly the cranial and accessory lobes. Thymus appeared small in 6 of 10 males and 5 of 10 females. No treatment-related macroscopic findings were observed in any other organ or tissue."
