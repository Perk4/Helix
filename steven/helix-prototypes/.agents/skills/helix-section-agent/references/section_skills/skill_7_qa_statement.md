# Skill: Section 7 — Quality Assurance Statement
# Sources: approved_report_1, approved_report_2, approved_report_3
# Primary data source: ProtocolContext (or QA audit log if available)

## Purpose
States GLP compliance and documents QA audit inspections. This section is mandatory for every GLP report (§58.35, §58.185). Content is largely templated — the QA statement sentence is fixed, and the audit table is filled from known or supplied audit dates.

## Structure (always in this order)
1. GLP compliance statement (one sentence, fixed)
2. Audit table (one row per phase inspected)

## GLP Compliance Statement (fixed — do not vary)
"This study was conducted in compliance with GLP regulations (21 CFR Part 58). Periodic inspections were conducted by the Quality Assurance Unit."

## Audit Table — Required Columns
Phase Inspected | Audit Date | Reporting Date

## Standard Audit Phases (use these labels)
For 28-day studies (minimum 1 phase):
  - "In-life phase"
  - "Pathology review"

For 13-week studies (minimum 3 phases):
  - "In-life phase (Weeks 1–4)"
  - "In-life phase (Weeks 9–13)"
  - "Recovery phase" (if recovery groups)
  - "Pathology review"

## Date Format
DD Month YYYY: "06 September 2024", not "09/06/2024" or "2024-09-06"
Reporting date is typically 7 days after audit date.

## What Must Not Be Missing
- GLP compliance statement must be present (R-4 eval rule)
- At minimum one audit phase with a date (R-4 eval rule)
- Reporting date for each audit phase

## Example (from approved_report_3)
"This study was conducted in compliance with GLP regulations (21 CFR Part 58). Periodic inspections were conducted by the Quality Assurance Unit.

| Phase Inspected | Audit Date | Reporting Date |
|---|---|---|
| In-life phase | 06 September 2024 | 13 September 2024 |
| Pathology review | 24 September 2024 | 01 October 2024 |"
