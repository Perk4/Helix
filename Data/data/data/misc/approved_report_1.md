# NONCLINICAL STUDY REPORT

**Study Title:** A 28-Day Repeat-Dose Oral Gavage Toxicity Study with Compound XR-247 in Sprague-Dawley Rats
**Study Number:** TOX-2024-0412
**Test Facility:** Nonclinical Safety Research, New Brunswick, NJ
**Sponsor:** Internal — Nonclinical Safety Research
**Test Article:** Compound XR-247
**Species/Strain:** Rat / Sprague-Dawley (Crl:CD(SD))
**GLP Status:** GLP
**Study Director:** A. Reynolds, PhD
**Report Date:** 14 March 2024
**Report Status:** APPROVED

---

## Summary

Compound XR-247 was administered once daily by oral gavage to Sprague-Dawley rats (5/sex/group) at doses of 0, 10, 50, or 200 mg/kg/day for 28 consecutive days. All animals survived to scheduled necropsy. No treatment-related clinical signs were observed at 0 or 10 mg/kg/day.

At 50 mg/kg/day, transient soft stool was noted in 3 of 10 animals during Days 8–14. Minimal hepatocellular hypertrophy (Grade 1) was present in the liver of 3 of 5 males and 2 of 5 females; this change was considered adaptive and non-adverse. Body weight gain was mildly reduced (males: −6.0%; females: −3.6% vs. controls at Day 28). Liver weights were slightly increased. No macroscopic liver abnormalities were observed.

At 200 mg/kg/day, multiple treatment-related clinical signs were present throughout the study including reduced activity, hunched posture, soft stool, and partially closed eyes. Body weight gain was markedly reduced in males (−13.9%) and females (−9.9%) by Day 28. Liver weights were substantially increased in males (33.5% above control) and females (36.3% above control). Grossly, livers appeared enlarged and pale tan in all Group 4 animals. Microscopically, moderate hepatocellular hypertrophy (Grade 2–3) and mild centrilobular necrosis (Grade 1–2) were present in the liver of all Group 4 animals. Minimal renal tubular basophilia (Grade 1) was observed in 3 of 5 males and 2 of 5 females. Thymic lymphoid depletion (Grade 1) was observed in 2 of 5 Group 4 males. All findings at 200 mg/kg/day are considered adverse and attributable to compound administration.

**The no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) for Compound XR-247 under the conditions of this study is 10 mg/kg/day.** The lowest-observed-adverse-effect level (LOAEL) is 200 mg/kg/day.

---

## 1. Objective

The objectives of this study were to: (1) evaluate the potential toxicity of Compound XR-247 following repeated oral gavage administration to Sprague-Dawley rats for 28 consecutive days; (2) characterise the nature, severity, and dose-response relationship of any treatment-related effects; (3) identify target organs of toxicity; and (4) establish a no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) to support further development. The study was conducted in compliance with Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) regulations.

Compound XR-247 is a small-molecule candidate under development for treatment of chronic inflammatory disease. Based on pharmacological activity in prior in vitro screens, the liver was identified as a potential target organ. The doses selected for this study (10, 50, and 200 mg/kg/day) were based on the maximum tolerated dose established in a 7-day range-finding study.

## 3. Materials and Methods

### 3.1 Test Article

Compound XR-247 was supplied by the Sponsor Analytical Chemistry Group. The batch used (XR247-2023-B01) had a purity of 98.7% (HPLC). The test article was stored at controlled room temperature, protected from light.

### 3.2 Vehicle

Vehicle was 0.5% methylcellulose (w/v) in purified water. Dosing formulations were prepared as a suspension weekly and stored refrigerated (2 to 8°C).

### 3.3 Test System

Naive, virus antibody-free Sprague-Dawley rats (Crl:CD(SD)) were obtained from Charles River Laboratories. Animals arrived at 7 weeks of age and were acclimated for a minimum of 5 days prior to Day 1. Animals were assigned to groups by stratified randomisation based on Day 1 body weight.

### 3.4 Dose Formulation Verification

Dosing formulation samples were collected for concentration verification on Day 1 and during Week 4. Samples were analysed by HPLC. Acceptance criteria: measured concentration within ±10% of nominal.

### 3.5 Terminal Procedures

All surviving animals were euthanised on Day 29 by carbon dioxide inhalation followed by exsanguination. A complete macroscopic examination was performed on all animals. Terminal body weights were recorded immediately prior to euthanasia. The following organs were weighed: liver, kidneys (paired), heart, brain, spleen, and thymus.

### 3.6 Histology and Histopathology

Tissues from all animals in Groups 1 and 4 and target tissues (liver, kidney) from Groups 2 and 3 were fixed in 10% neutral buffered formalin, processed, and embedded in paraffin. Sections were cut at 4 to 6 μm and stained with haematoxylin and eosin (H&E). Microscopic findings were graded on a scale of 1 (Minimal) to 5 (Severe). A pathology peer review was conducted by an independent board-certified veterinary pathologist.

### 3.7 Statistical Analysis

Continuous variables (body weights, organ weights) were analysed by one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's post-hoc test. Where Bartlett's test indicated heterogeneity of variance (p < 0.05), the Kruskal-Wallis test with Dunn's post-hoc was applied. Incidence data were compared by Fisher's exact test. Statistical significance was set at p < 0.05.

## 5. Results

### 5.1 Test Article Formulation and Analyses Verification

Dosing formulation samples were collected and analysed on Day 1 and during Week 4. All measured concentrations were within ±10% of the nominal target concentration. No test article was detected in vehicle control formulations. All dosing formulations met the predefined acceptance criteria.

### 5.2 In-Life Observations and Measurements

#### 5.2.1 Mortality

No unscheduled deaths or moribund euthanasias occurred during the study. All 40 animals (20 males, 20 females) survived to scheduled necropsy on Day 29.

#### 5.2.2 Clinical Observations

Cage-side observations were recorded twice daily throughout the study. Detailed clinical examinations were performed weekly.

**Group 1 (0 mg/kg/day — Vehicle Control):** No treatment-related clinical signs were observed in any male or female animal during the study.

**Group 2 (10 mg/kg/day — Low Dose):** No treatment-related clinical signs were observed. All animals appeared healthy throughout the dosing period.

**Group 3 (50 mg/kg/day — Mid Dose):** Soft stool was observed in 3 of 10 animals (M301, M303, and M305) during Days 8 to 14. The finding was transient and resolved without intervention by Day 15. No other clinical signs were noted.

**Group 4 (200 mg/kg/day — High Dose):** Multiple clinical signs consistent with systemic toxicity were observed throughout the study. Reduced activity was observed in 6 of 10 animals (M401, M402, M403, M404, F401, F402) from Day 1 through Day 28. Hunched posture was recorded in 4 of 10 animals (M401, M402, M403, F401) from Day 1 through Day 21. Soft stool was observed in 7 of 10 animals from Day 1 through Day 28. Partially closed eyes were noted in 3 animals (M401, M402, F401) from Days 1 to 14. All clinical signs persisted for the duration of dosing and are considered treatment-related.

Body weight gain in Group 4 males was reduced by 13.9% relative to controls by Day 28 (291.5 g vs. 338.7 g). Group 4 females showed a similar reduction of 9.9% (220.6 g vs. 244.8 g). Group 3 showed mild reductions of 6.0% (males) and 3.6% (females) relative to controls at Day 28. Groups 1 and 2 showed comparable body weight trajectories throughout the study.

Liver weights were statistically increased in Group 3 (12.82 g males, 9.66 g females) and Group 4 (15.33 g males, 11.79 g females) relative to controls. The increase in Group 4 males represents a 33.5% elevation over the control mean (p < 0.01, Dunnett's test). Thymus weights were reduced in Group 4 (males: 0.28 g; females: 0.30 g), consistent with stress or lymphoid depletion. All other organ weights were comparable across groups.

#### 5.3.2 Macroscopic Observations

A complete macroscopic examination was performed on all animals at scheduled necropsy on Day 29.

**Groups 1 and 2 (0 and 10 mg/kg/day):** No treatment-related macroscopic findings were observed in any tissue in male or female animals.

**Group 3 (50 mg/kg/day):** No macroscopic findings were noted in the liver or any other tissue. Microscopic hepatocellular hypertrophy at this dose was not associated with a macroscopic correlate.

**Group 4 (200 mg/kg/day):** The liver appeared enlarged and pale tan in all 5 males and all 5 females. In 2 of 5 males (M401, M402), dark surface foci were additionally noted on the liver, consistent with focal necrosis observed microscopically. No macroscopic findings were noted in the kidney, thymus, or any other tissue.

#### 5.3.3 Microscopic Findings

Microscopic examination was performed on tissues from all animals in Groups 1 and 4 and target tissues (liver, kidney) from Groups 2 and 3.

**Liver:**

Groups 1 and 2: No treatment-related microscopic findings. Hepatic parenchyma appeared within normal limits in all animals examined.

Group 3: Minimal hepatocellular hypertrophy (Grade 1) was observed in 3 of 5 males (M301, M302, M304) and 2 of 5 females (F301, F303). The finding consisted of enlarged hepatocytes with abundant pale cytoplasm, predominantly in the centrilobular region. This change is consistent with hepatic enzyme induction and is considered adaptive rather than adverse at this dose.

Group 4: Moderate hepatocellular hypertrophy (Grade 2 to 3) was present in all 5 males and all 5 females. Additionally, mild centrilobular necrosis (Grade 1 to 2) was observed in 2 of 5 males (M401, M402) and 1 of 5 females (F401). Necrosis was characterised by hepatocyte loss and karyolysis in the centrilobular zone. These findings are considered adverse and directly attributable to compound administration.

**Kidneys:**

Groups 1 and 2: No treatment-related findings.

Group 3: No treatment-related renal findings.

Group 4: Minimal renal tubular basophilia (Grade 1) was observed in 3 of 5 males (M401, M402, M404) and 2 of 5 females (F402, F403). The finding consisted of basophilic tubular epithelial cells in the cortex, consistent with tubular regeneration following mild nephrotoxic injury.

**Thymus:**

No treatment-related thymic findings were observed in Groups 1, 2, or 3. Thymic lymphoid depletion (Grade 1) was observed in 2 of 5 Group 4 males (M401, M402), consistent with stress-related involution secondary to body weight loss and systemic toxicity.

**Other Organs (Heart, Brain, Spleen, Adrenal Glands, Gonads, Lungs, Bone Marrow):**

No treatment-related microscopic findings were identified in the heart, brain, spleen, adrenal glands, gonads, lungs, or bone marrow in any dose group.

#### 5.3.4 Conclusion

The no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) for Compound XR-247 under the conditions of this study is **10 mg/kg/day**, based on the absence of adverse findings at the low dose level. The lowest-observed-adverse-effect level (LOAEL) is **200 mg/kg/day**, based on adverse hepatic findings (hepatocellular hypertrophy Grade 2–3 and centrilobular necrosis), renal tubular basophilia, body weight reduction, and clinical signs of systemic toxicity.
